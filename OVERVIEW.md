# Press Play Anime

The public streaming and crowdfunding site for Press Play International Media, an anime licensing and localization company. It's currently in closed beta.

**Who it's for**
- **Viewers** sign up on the site. Free viewers see ads before episodes. Premium viewers pay $8/month and watch without ads. Both can manage their own profile, billing and account.
- **Advertisers** (invite only) upload video ads, which go live after admin approval. They can then track delivery against their target views.
- **Licensors** (invite only) get read-only views and revenue-share analytics for their own titles.
- **Admins** have full control: users (ban, delete, change tier), content, ad approval, crowdfunding, news, coupons and referrals, invites, the mailing list, and which sections each audience can see. Admin accounts can't be deleted; admins can promote others to Admin or Moderator.
- **Moderators** (invite or promoted by an admin) manage content, episode uploads, ads, crowdfunding and news, but not users, invites, coupons, the mailing list or settings.

The whole site can be switched between English and Japanese from the menu.

**Purpose**
Until our own licensed titles launch, the home page shows every video from our YouTube channel. The site also hosts Kickstarter-style campaigns that fans fund to greenlight new releases.
